# ApprenticeOps Research Radar — 2026-07-13

> **Scope honesty.** This is a candidate-evidence scan, not a paper amendment.
> Company reports are first-party evidence; practitioner posts are leads. No
> timeout-run result, bibliography entry, or paper claim was changed.

## Scope

We scanned work first published or materially revised between 2025-01-01 and
2026-07-13. The target is small, specialized, or efficient language-model work
that could change ApprenticeOps' experiments or analysis: PEFT/LoRA, fine-tuning,
distillation, synthetic data, quantization, reasoning budgets, on-device/CPU
systems, safety, evaluation, and operations benchmarks.

The evidence ledger contains **29 query records**, **42 immutable source
versions**, and **42 scoped claim versions**. Search coverage includes all ten
standing topics, all nine required company families, primary indexes, company
research, model/code artifacts, independent reproductions, and social discovery.

## What Was Already Covered

The existing catalog already covers the central SLM-agent argument, the broad SLM
survey, ThinkSLM, Mamba, efficient-reasoning overthinking, 4-bit scaling, LLM in
a Flash, energy-per-inference evidence, AIOpsLab, ITBench, and the current
reasoning/quantization/agent-safety cluster. We did not relabel those papers as
new.

The new scan primarily extends four areas that were thin or absent:

1. **Adaptation integrity:** contamination, generalization, retention, and safety
   after fine-tuning.
2. **Specialists as explicit treatments:** task adapters, function-specific
   models, and bounded adapter banks.
3. **Deployment identity:** runtime, parser, template, quantization method, and
   total/resident parameters as part of the experimental condition.
4. **Recovery-aware operations evaluation:** valid action and restored health,
   not diagnosis alone.

## New, Updated, and Contradictory Evidence

### Specialized small models are becoming a real design class

FunctionGemma is the clearest narrow-specialist example: Google reports a
58%→85% Mobile Actions lift after task fine-tuning and gives a measured Samsung
CPU profile. It is also explicit that the 270M model is a foundation for
fine-tuning, not a general dialogue model. Apple exposes a similar architecture
at a larger scale—an approximately 3B on-device model with QAT and LoRA
application adapters—but no public weights. Granite H-Micro and SmolLM3 provide
open 3B artifacts with tool calling; MobileLLM-R1 supplies a sub-billion
reasoning recipe; Ministral 3 uses cascade distillation.

These sources support testing **specialist versus generalist deployment
packages**, not claiming that a specialist replaces a generalist. Utility must be
measured beside retention, restraint, safety, latency, energy, and routing errors.

### Fine-tuning is useful and unsafe in ways that do not collapse into one score

RandLoRA shows that LoRA's low rank can itself constrain adaptation. K-Merge
shows a bounded continual-adapter bank is technically plausible on 1B–1.5B
models. But the strongest safety evidence is adversarial: narrow insecure-code
fine-tuning can produce broad misalignment, and even influence-selected benign
samples can break alignment. Safe Delta is a mitigation candidate, not a waiver
for evaluation.

The correct ApprenticeOps unit is therefore not “fine-tuned model score.” It is a
paired deployment record:

- base checkpoint;
- adapter/training-data/teacher lineage;
- in-domain utility;
- incident-disjoint utility;
- general-capability retention;
- full safety suite;
- latency, memory, and energy.

### Distillation can transfer capability, contamination, or both

Apple's distillation scaling law says distillation is attractive when a teacher
already exists or serves many students, but not necessarily when one teacher is
trained for one student. Data Laundering demonstrates the evaluation hazard:
benchmark-specific teacher knowledge can enter a tiny student without genuine
reasoning. Synthetic-data evidence is similarly conditional: replacing real data
can collapse, while accumulating real and synthetic data remained stable in the
tested workflows.

Teacher identity, source-example lineage, benchmark overlap, and retained real
or replay data are load-bearing provenance fields.

### Quantization and sparse activation have no universal shortcut

ParetoQ weakens any universal “4-bit optimum” statement: trained 2-bit or ternary
models can be competitive under suitable QAT and kernels. The Raspberry Pi study
also finds model-specific q3/q4/q8 latency and energy ordering. ACBench shows
compression loss varies by capability. The edge-MoE study directly warns against
using active parameters as realized edge cost: total model footprint and memory
pressure can erase the FLOP advantage.

The local conclusion remains narrower and testable: **q4 is a useful prior on the
current node, not a law.** Exact-lineage, same-runtime q3/q4/q8 comparisons are
needed before changing it.

### Operations evaluation is moving from answers to state transitions

The new operations cluster is unusually aligned with ApprenticeOps. STRATUS adds
an explicit state machine and Transactional No-Regression. AgenticOpsEval scores
localization, identification, and evidence. R2Act shows that high diagnosis
accuracy can coexist with low recovery validity. Cloud-OpsBench offers
reproducible state snapshots. The RCA failure study identifies incomplete
exploration and hallucinated telemetry as cross-model process failures. PROBE
uses structured evidence and bounded guidance after failure.

This is a stronger direction than adding more prose-only diagnosis questions.
The next operations pack should represent:

`evidence → diagnosis → operation → target → parameters → preconditions → expected postcondition → execution receipt → observed health`.

### Contradictory and qualifying findings

- **Contamination:** paraphrasing is not a reliable clean holdout, but old
  contamination may be forgotten after very long continued training. The latter
  does not validate short LoRA/SFT runs.
- **SFT:** SFT is useful for output stabilization but did not generalize like
  outcome-reward RL in the tested variants. In-domain SFT lift is not sufficient
  evidence of new capability.
- **Adapter merging:** recycling public LoRAs rarely beat a target-task LoRA;
  random adapters behaved similarly unless highly relevant adapters were present.
- **Reasoning:** longer traces can help, but token caps can trigger severe accuracy
  collapse. Reasoning mode and budget must remain separate conditions.

## Trends and Disagreements

1. **The deployment package is replacing the bare model as the unit of analysis.**
   Weight revision, quantization method, runtime, parser, chat template, reasoning
   mode, and tool schema can each move observed behavior.
2. **Specialization is becoming narrower and more explicit.** Function-specific
   models and typed adapters are credible, but route selection and out-of-domain
   restraint become first-class failure modes.
3. **Fine-tuning quality is not a monotonic utility knob.** Utility, safety,
   retention, contamination, and calibration can move independently.
4. **Synthetic data is a workflow, not a substance.** Teacher, filtering,
   accumulation/replacement, and overlap determine whether it helps or launders a
   benchmark.
5. **Hardware-aware conclusions remain local.** QAT, q-level, sparse activation,
   and hybrid architecture ordering changes with kernels and devices.
6. **Operations work increasingly distinguishes diagnosis from recovery.** This
   is the most actionable gap for ApprenticeOps.
7. **No accepted protocol was found for timeout/token-budget censoring in LLM
   benchmarks.** ApprenticeOps' separate failure strata remain a defensible
   method contribution, not yet a literature-backed standard.

## Gaps ApprenticeOps Can Fill

### 1. A preregistered adaptation study for sovereign ops

No reviewed paper jointly measures specialist utility, incident-disjoint
transfer, general retention, destructive-action safety, contamination, latency,
and energy for a small local ops model. ApprenticeOps can fill this without
claiming a new fine-tuning algorithm.

### 2. Exact-lineage quantization under operational tasks

The literature still lacks a matched q3/q4/q8 CPU study that includes open ops
reasoning, safety, parse failures, and direct energy. Existing evidence suggests
the ordering is hardware- and capability-specific.

### 3. Diagnosis-to-recovery validity for small offline models

R2Act and PROBE establish the diagnosis/recovery gap for larger agent systems.
ApprenticeOps can ask whether a ≤5B local model can choose an admissible bounded
action after a correct diagnosis, without granting it mutation authority.

### 4. RAG × training-type interaction on the same incident

The system-log paper reports spectacular RAG gains for tiny SLMs and degradation
for some reasoners, but on a narrow classification task. ApprenticeOps already
needs paired with/without-reference variants; adding training type and latency
would make the result causal enough to interpret.

### 5. Failure-inclusive evaluation of reasoning budgets

No reviewed benchmark jointly models timeout, token-cap length, blank output,
parse failure, OOM, and completion as separate outcomes. The current recovery
work can become a methodological comparison after it is complete and locked.

### 6. A second hardware point

Almost every edge study remains one or two devices. Reproducing a narrow model
panel on a second x86 or ARM node would improve external validity more than adding
another 100 model tags on the same ThinkPad.

## Candidate Experiments

These are proposals, not findings. None should start while the timeout study is
active.

| Priority | Experiment | Minimal arms | Primary gate |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Paired adaptation study** | base; paired RAG; target LoRA; replay-anchored LoRA; safety-defended LoRA | Incident-disjoint utility improves with no material safety/retention regression. |
| 2 | **Diagnosis-to-action validity** | diagnosis only; typed action; typed action + bounded evidence guidance | Correct diagnosis does not count unless operation/target are admissible and postcondition is testable. |
| 3 | **Exact-lineage quantization** | q3, q4, q8 of one or two lineages on one runtime | Failure-inclusive quality × safety × direct energy, paired by scenario/repetition. |
| 4 | **RAG × training type** | same incident, reference absent/present; instruct/reasoning | Paired lift, latency, length/timeout, contradiction rate. |
| 5 | **Tool-filtered inference** | baseline; best-of-3; deterministic filter then semantic rank | Equal total-token or equal-energy comparison, not free extra compute. |
| 6 | **Specialist routing** | FunctionGemma-like specialist; small generalist; specialist→generalist fallback | Format, correct call, restraint, multi-turn state, and end-to-end completion scored separately. |
| 7 | **BitNet runtime condition** | native BitNet runtime versus same-size dense q4/q8 comparators | Treat architecture/training/runtime as a package; no causal quantization claim. |
| 8 | **Storage-bounded adapter bank** | target LoRA; K-Merge-like bank; randomized-adapter control | Retention, imperfect routing, safety, storage, and merge latency. |

Training can happen on a separate capable machine; the claim-bearing inference
and telemetry must return to the locked sovereign CPU environment. That boundary
must be explicit.

## Analysis Implications

1. **Use a deployment-condition hierarchy.** Model family, training path,
   adapter, quantization, runtime, template, parser, and reasoning mode are nested
   or crossed factors—not labels to collapse into “model.”
2. **Pair whenever possible.** Compare base/tuned/defended or q3/q4/q8 within the
   same lineage and scenario/repetition.
3. **Separate conditional and unconditional outcomes.** Report quality among
   completed responses and failure-inclusive quality with timeout, length, blank,
   OOM, parse, and tool failures separate.
4. **Use incident-disjoint and time-disjoint evaluation.** Paraphrases are not a
   sufficient contamination defense. Add canaries and teacher/dataset lineage.
5. **Pre-register non-inferiority safety and retention margins.** A specialist
   lift is not acceptable if it silently erodes broad capability or refusal.
6. **Report risk-coverage out of fold.** Repeat disagreement, check-vector
   instability, and abstention are candidate uncertainty signals; thresholds must
   be selected away from test incidents.
7. **Do not collapse trade-offs into one score.** Keep utility, safety,
   reliability, latency, energy, and storage visible; use Pareto/SMAA only after
   each axis is valid.
8. **Distinguish measured from estimated systems data.** Record hardware,
   runtime commit, threads, precision, prompt/decode lengths, repetitions, direct
   or estimated energy source, and uncertainty.
9. **Treat company claims and independent reproductions as different evidence
   strata.** They can agree without becoming the same source.

## Negative Results

The scan found no qualifying primary study that:

- formalizes timeout, OOM, blank output, token-cap length, and completion as
  competing or censored LLM-benchmark outcomes;
- defines an accepted tokenizer-fairness protocol for constrained hardware;
- jointly evaluates CPU-local personalization across utility, retention, safety,
  contamination, latency, and energy;
- compares dense and MoE models inside a ≤5B total-parameter boundary on an older
  x86 CPU;
- independently reproduces the headline CPU/energy claims of the selected major
  company releases.

These are search results, not proofs of non-existence.

## Limitations

- The scan is broad but not exhaustive. Search APIs, pages, and venue indexes can
  omit or reorder results.
- Several 2026 papers are recent preprints. Venue, artifacts, and claims may
  change; version IDs preserve the observed state.
- Company model cards and technical blogs are not independent evidence.
- Most adaptation training is GPU-scale; CPU feasibility often means
  post-evaluating a released checkpoint, not reproducing training.
- Social and issue-tracker evidence is configuration-sensitive and may be
  incomplete.
- The scan directly verified primary pages and pinned artifacts, but did not
  reproduce any model result.
- The active timeout study was not inspected for partial treatment effects and
  was not modified.
