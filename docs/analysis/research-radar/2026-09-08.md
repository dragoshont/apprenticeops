## Analysis Implications

1. **Lock denominators first.** The intended-attempt population must include unsuccessful and unscorable attempts. Report usability/completion probability and quality conditional on valid completion separately. Also show a pre-declared failure-inclusive utility view; do not silently replace missing terminal states with success.
2. **Separate failure types from censoring.** A deadline can right-censor latent time-to-completion, while the same observation is an observed operational failure under that deadline. OOM, parser failure and blank output are different terminal events. [ENAMEL] is older precedent for censored execution time, **not a ready-made estimator for all these events**. Competing-risks or survival analysis is optional and requires defensible event/censoring assumptions; a transparent status table is preferable to unjustified imputation.
3. **Define energy per usable answer transparently.** For a fixed workload and declared usability rule, propose `sum(measured attempt energy) / number of usable answers`, retaining failed-attempt energy. It is not energy averaged only over successes. With zero usable answers, report undefined/infinite cost and the numerator, not zero or a hidden cap. Repeat across defensible thresholds and bootstrap numerator and denominator jointly by scenario. This is **not a novelty claim**.
4. **Keep power boundaries visible.** Package RAPL is not wall/system energy and can exclude DRAM. Missing energy is not zero. Do not pool package and platform domains or controlled and dynamic-frequency regimes. [HYDRA]/[PROMPT] justify instrumentation care, not a conversion factor.
5. **Replace compromised validation, not merely its weights.** A disagreement-enriched set is not a full-corpus probability sample without a defensible design. Inclusion weighting cannot restore omitted context or undo prior exposure to expected scores. The parent reports context capped at 1,800 characters and answers at 2,500: verify complete relevant task input and the entire scored answer in a fresh display, with automated scores/model identities hidden. Keep prior ratings as labelled pilot/diagnostic data, not automatic held-out gold. Specify rater qualifications, exposure, presentation and criteria [HUMAN]; assess design-aware human estimates before optional PPI [PPI]/[ARES]. [OMISSION] warns that missing evidence can evade a holistic score, but does not quantify this packet's clipping bias.
6. **Keep paired structure and bootstrap multiplicities.** Repeated runs and two ratings of one answer are not independent tasks. A resample is a multiset: drawing scenario IDs `[A,A,B]` must preserve two copies of A, via distinct draw IDs or explicit weights, all the way through grouping. Regrouping on original IDs is not automatically wrong; collapsing copies and then averaging distinct groups equally is wrong for that intended resample. Test this before trusting scenario CIs. [BOOTSTRAP] supplies older crossed-weight methodology, not a universal small-cluster coverage guarantee or a mandate for multiway resampling.
7. **Define `pass_1` before reporting a reliability gap.** In [TAU], `pass^1` is task-averaged per-trial binary success, not an indicator that the average ordinal score crosses a threshold. Let `Y[s,r]` be the declared per-answer usable-success label; the equal-scenario statistic is the average across scenarios of `sum_r Y[s,r] / n_s`. Thresholding must occur at the intended per-answer unit before repeat aggregation. The all-k-success estimator is task-averaged `choose(c_s,k)/choose(n_s,k)` under its stated trial assumptions; with exactly k observed repeats it reduces to the all-repeats-pass indicator. Do not substitute the kth power of a pooled mean or confuse `pass^k` with at-least-one-success `pass@k`.
8. **Do not “complete” deployment failures by matrix completion.** [COLLAB] concerns planned sparse evaluation and inference assumptions, not informative failure. [PPI] also does not excuse missing human validation.
9. **Treat parser, template, cache and quantization as provenance.** [CACHE] provides direct accelerator evidence and an offline scorer-correction precedent. Current CPU effects remain to be assessed from actual logs; absence of required runtime metadata is itself a limitation.
