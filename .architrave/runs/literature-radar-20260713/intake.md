# Intake

## Understanding

Build a recurring, delta-oriented radar for recent small-model research and
practitioner signals. Each scan must reconcile against ApprenticeOps' existing
catalog, separate primary evidence from social leads, and turn changed evidence
into grounded gaps, experiments, analysis ideas, and research directions.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Cover the fixed topic, organization, and source-family matrix, including
	explicit zero-result searches.
2. Record immutable source versions and claim-level update/contradiction lineage.
3. Label each source `already-covered`, `new`, `updated`, or `contradictory`.
4. Corroborate social/practitioner leads before using them outside the Social
	Leads section.
5. Produce a dated report, an on-demand skill, durable ledgers, schemas, and a
	stdlib validator with focused tests.
6. Keep the active timeout experiment and canonical paper/bibliography untouched.
7. Separate actual reviewer feedback from anticipated questions and internal
	audit findings.
8. Produce a source-traceable paper-impact packet with add-now,
	add-after-current-run, related-work-only, future-work, monitor, and reject
	dispositions.
9. Preserve the packet without promoting a source or editing claim-bearing
	manuscript text.

## Assumptions

- Prioritize 2025-2026; include older work only to establish a trend or conflict.
- Company/model-card evidence is first-party, not independent validation.
- Social sources are discovery leads, never scientific ground truth by themselves.

## Blocking Questions

None. This run is research-only and does not inspect treatment outcomes.
